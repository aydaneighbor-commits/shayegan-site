07B Shayegan Store

Subscriptions for the Shayegan app (coming soon) and this site. How it works: you register with your own email and choose your own password, pick Bronze / Gold / Diamond, and payment activates the subscription on your own account. We never sell or hand out pre-made Gmail accounts or passwords — your login is yours alone.

1 · Register

Create your Shayegan account with your own email and a unique username.

2 · Choose a tier

Bronze is free. Gold and Diamond unlock AI-film downloads, early access and member perks.

3 · Activation

After payment, your tier is activated on your account and you can download your personal license.txt — a text file with your own Shayegan activation key for the app. It never contains a password or any third-party login.
